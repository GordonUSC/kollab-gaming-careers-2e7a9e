Your first steps

Start this week. Start small.

You don't need a gaming PC. You need to finish one small thing.

  1. Make something tiny. One level, one sound, one character, one choice.
  2. Share it with one person. Ask: "What did you feel?" Then make it better.
  3. Join a game jam. Teams make a game in a weekend. Free, online, beginners welcome.
  4. Talk to someone who does the job you circled. Ask one question (see below).
  5. Save everything you make. That's the start of your portfolio.

Free tools

Questions to ask anyone who works in games

  • What does a normal day look like for you?
  • What did you make before your first job?
  • What would you tell yourself at my age?
  • Who should I talk to next?
